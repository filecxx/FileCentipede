Privacy Statement

Last Updated: October 22, 2026 (Possibly Never)

  • 1. Privacy Commitment

    We take user privacy seriously and solemnly commit that this software does not collect, upload, share, or sell any personal information or usage data, nor does it send user data to the developer or any third party.

  • 2. No Collection of User Information

    This software has no functionality designed to collect personal information, usage behavior, or other user data.

    We do not collect, record, or track your usage, download history, browsing history, device information, IP address, or any other information that could be used to identify you or understand your behavior.

  • 3. No Data Uploads

    This software does not send user information, usage statistics, telemetry data, diagnostic reports, or crash reports to the developer's servers or any other servers.

    The software contains no background mechanisms designed to collect or upload user data, nor does it transmit user data to external services without your explicit action.

  • 4. Local Data Processing

    The software processes download tasks, task history, configuration settings, and other data required for its operation locally. Such data is managed on your device and is not uploaded to the developer's servers by the software.

    We do not collect or upload your download history, file lists, download paths, device identifiers, usage statistics, diagnostic information, or any other data related to your usage of the software.

  • 5. P2P Network Communication and Privacy

    This software supports BitTorrent and other P2P download protocols. To provide these features, the software may need to communicate with other network peers, Tracker servers, and the DHT network.

    Such communications are necessary for the underlying P2P protocols and related networking features to function. They do not mean that the software reports your activities to the developer or collects your data.

    Please be aware of the following:

    • DHT: The software may send queries to the DHT network and exchange peer addresses and other protocol-related data to discover download peers. DHT can be disabled in the software.
    • Trackers: The software may send information required by the protocol to Tracker servers, including torrent identifiers, client ports, and downloaded or remaining data amounts.
    • PEX: When Peer Exchange is enabled, the software may exchange known peer addresses with other peers.
    • Peer Connections: When establishing P2P connections, your public IP address and connection port may become visible to other participating peers.
    • Other Protocol Communications: Depending on the download protocol and configuration, the software may exchange information necessary for downloading, peer discovery, and connection management.

    These exchanges are determined by the relevant network protocols and features. Third-party peers and servers may record or process the information they receive. Such activities are beyond the control of the software's developer.

    The software does not independently collect this P2P network communication data, aggregate it, upload it, or report it to the developer.

  • 6. No Telemetry or Tracking

    This software contains no telemetry, usage analytics, behavioral tracking, or advertising tracking features designed to report user activities to the developer.

    The software does not send data to the developer or any designated collection endpoint to count users, analyze user behavior, or monitor download activities.

  • 7. Privacy and the Network Environment

    Although this software does not actively collect or upload user data to the developer, P2P networks are not anonymous by nature. Other participating peers, Tracker servers, Internet service providers, and other parties capable of observing relevant network traffic may obtain or infer certain information through normal network communications.

    You should choose your downloads, network settings, and connection methods according to your privacy requirements. This software does not promise to hide your IP address or make P2P activities completely anonymous.

  • Summary

    The above sections provide a formal overview of this software's privacy practices. To put it simply, the developer will not waste time designing features to collect user information, let alone play word games or use excuses such as "anonymous uploads" or "improving the product experience" to justify collecting user data.

    This project is a personal hobby project. It was originally created simply to make the developer's own everyday file downloads more convenient. The developer has no interest in profiting from user data, nor the time or desire to turn this project into a commercial product that relies on collecting user data.

    In fact, the developer barely has the time or motivation to maintain this rather modest website.

    Software is meant to solve problems, not collect user information.
